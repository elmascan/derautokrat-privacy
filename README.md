# Der Autokrat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

## Overview
Der Autokrat ("the app") is a mobile card game with language 
learning features developed by an independent developer.

## Data We Collect
**We do not collect any personal data.**

The app does not require account registration, does not collect 
names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information.

## Data Stored Locally
The following data is stored only on your device 
(never sent to any server):
- Your selected language and difficulty settings
- Vocabulary words you save during gameplay
- Your best score

This data can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app.

## Advertising
This app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.
Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data 
to show relevant ads. For EU/EEA users, consent will be 
requested before any personalised advertising.

For more information about Google's data practices:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children
This app is rated for ages 12 and above.
We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
